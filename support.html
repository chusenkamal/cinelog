Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. CineLog has no account and no cloud sync, so your watchlist and diary never leave the device. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or move my diary?

Open Diary → Settings (gear) → Export Diary. You'll get a CSV file you can save to Files, open in Numbers, or import into Letterboxd.

A title shows no streaming services

Streaming availability comes from JustWatch through TMDB and is specific to your country. Some titles, especially new or older ones, may not be available to stream in your region.

Search shows "You're offline"

Searching, browsing and title details need an internet connection. Your watchlist and diary still work offline.

How do I delete everything?

Open Diary → Settings → Erase All Data. This permanently removes your watchlist, diary and recent searches from the device.

Accessibility

CineLog supports VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, light and dark appearance, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion. If something isn't working well with your accessibility settings, please get in touch.

CineLog uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
