CineLog Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Data Not Collected CineLog doesn't collect any data about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your diary is stored

Your watchlist, watch dates, ratings and reviews are stored on your device. If iCloud Sync is on (Diary → Settings → iCloud Sync), they're also kept in your own private iCloud database so your other devices stay up to date. That database belongs to your Apple Account: the developer can't see or access it. Recent searches stay on the device only.

You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time, and you can erase everything in Diary → Settings → Erase All Data, which also removes it from iCloud when sync is on. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device.

What CineLog asks The Movie Database (TMDB) for

To show movie and series information, CineLog sends requests to The Movie Database (TMDB):

  • the text you type into Search, and the IDs of titles you open, so TMDB can return results, details, cast and recommendations;
  • your device's region (for example "US"), so streaming availability matches your country.

These requests aren't linked to you: CineLog sends no name, account, advertising identifier or device identifier. As with any internet request, TMDB's servers can see your IP address. TMDB's handling of that is covered by the TMDB privacy policy. Posters and photos are loaded from TMDB's image servers.

Links that leave the app

Trailers open on YouTube, streaming links open on TMDB or JustWatch, and rating links open on IMDb or Letterboxd. Once you leave CineLog, those services' own privacy policies apply.

Exporting your diary

You can export your diary as a CSV file from Settings → Export Diary. The file goes only where you choose to share or save it.

Children

CineLog doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
